FAQ

BDT Retail questions, answered

What is BDT Retail?

BDT Retail is BuildDeploy Tech's cloud-based billing and retail management software. It handles GST billing and invoicing, POS, inventory, multi-store management and business reports from a single dashboard.

Is BDT Retail GST compliant?

Yes. You can create GST-ready invoices and generate GST, sales, purchase and profit reports whenever you need them.

Can I manage more than one store with BDT Retail?

Yes. BDT Retail supports multiple stores or outlets from a single central account, with staff roles and permissions for your team.

Does BDT Retail have a mobile app?

Yes. BDT Retail includes an Android mobile app so you can bill and keep track of your business on the go, alongside the web dashboard.

Which businesses is BDT Retail for?

BDT Retail works for retail and distribution businesses such as kirana and general stores, garments, footwear, electrical, hardware, cosmetics, mobile accessories, stationery, restaurants and wholesalers.

How do I get started or request a demo?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech through the contact page and the team will set up a demo and help you onboard your store.
