Billing + inventory

Why billing and inventory belong together

When billing and stock live in separate places, you end up counting stock by hand and finding out about shortages too late. In BDT Retail every bill and purchase updates your stock automatically, so your counter, your store room and your reports all agree.

That connection is what makes day-to-day retail easier: fewer manual updates, earlier low-stock warnings and numbers you can trust at the end of the day.

FAQ

BDT Retail questions, answered

What is BDT Retail?

BDT Retail is BuildDeploy Tech's cloud-based billing and retail management software for Indian businesses. It handles GST-ready billing and invoicing, POS, inventory, purchases, multi-store management and business reports from a single dashboard, with an Android app.

Can I create GST-ready invoices with BDT Retail?

Yes. You can set tax rates on your products and generate GST-ready invoices, along with GST, sales, purchase and profit reports. It helps you work in a GST-ready way; it does not replace advice from your accountant or tax professional.

Does BDT Retail support inventory and stock management?

Yes. BDT Retail tracks stock as you bill and purchase, shows low-stock alerts and helps you follow product movement across your catalogue.

Can I manage more than one store with BDT Retail?

Yes. BDT Retail supports multiple stores or outlets from a single central account, with staff roles and permissions so each team member sees only what they should.

Does BDT Retail support barcode billing?

Yes. BDT Retail supports barcodes so you can scan products for faster, more accurate billing at the counter.

Is there an Android app for BDT Retail?

Yes. BDT Retail includes an Android mobile app alongside the web dashboard, so you can bill and keep track of your business on the go.

Which businesses is BDT Retail for?

BDT Retail suits retail and distribution businesses such as kirana and general stores, garments, footwear, electrical, hardware, cosmetics, mobile accessories, stationery, restaurants and wholesalers.

How do I request a demo of BDT Retail?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech through the contact page by email or WhatsApp. The team will arrange a demo, answer your questions and help you set up your store.
