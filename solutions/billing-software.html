FAQ

Billing software questions, answered

What is billing and invoicing software?

Billing and invoicing software helps a business create invoices, take payments, apply taxes such as GST, and track sales and dues. BuildDeploy Tech builds this kind of software tailored to how your business works.

Do you build custom billing software or sell a ready-made product?

Both. We build custom billing and invoicing software around your specific workflow, and we also offer BDT Retail, our own ready-to-use cloud billing and POS product for retail businesses.

Is the billing software GST compliant?

Yes. We build GST-ready invoicing with consistent tax calculation across billing and reports, so your invoices and GST, sales and profit reports stay accurate.

Can you integrate payment gateways and our existing systems?

Yes. We can integrate payment gateways, accounting tools and other systems through APIs so your billing software fits into the tools you already use.

Can the software handle recurring or subscription billing?

Yes. We build recurring and subscription billing with automated invoices and renewals, alongside one-time and POS billing depending on your needs.

How do I get started or get a quote?
