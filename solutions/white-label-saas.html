FAQ

White-label SaaS questions, answered

What is white-label SaaS?

White-label SaaS is a ready-made software product you can rebrand and sell as your own. BuildDeploy Tech lets you launch a proven, multi-tenant SaaS platform under your own brand, domain and pricing.

What can I rebrand or customize?

You can apply your own logo, app name, custom domain, brand colors and pricing, so the product looks and feels like yours to your customers.

Which platform can I white-label?

You can white-label our multi-tenant billing and retail platform — the same foundations behind our own BDT Retail product — adapted to your brand and market.

How is white-label different from custom SaaS development?

White-label gets you to market faster and at lower cost by rebranding a proven platform, while custom SaaS development builds a brand-new product from scratch around a unique idea.

Do I control pricing and own the customers?

Yes. You set your own plans and pricing and own the customer relationship, while BuildDeploy Tech provides, hosts and maintains the underlying platform.

How do I get started with a white-label SaaS?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech with your brand and the market you want to serve, and the team will walk you through white-label options and share a quote.
