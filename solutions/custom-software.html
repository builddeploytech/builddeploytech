Custom vs off-the-shelf

Which makes sense for you?

Custom software is not always the right answer. Here's an honest comparison to help you decide.

FactorOff-the-shelf softwareCustom software
Workflow fitYou adapt to the productBuilt around your process
CustomisationLimited to settingsChanged to your needs
IntegrationsOnly what's offeredConnect what you need
OwnershipVendor-controlledYou own the solution
Upfront investmentLower to startHigher to build
Long-term costOngoing per-user feesBuild once, maintain
Time to startFast (sign up & use)Takes time to build
Best whenYour needs are standardYour process is specific or complex

If a standard product already covers your needs, it is often the faster, cheaper choice. Custom software tends to pay off when your process is specific, when you depend on integrations, or when generic tools are holding you back.

FAQ

Custom software questions, answered

What is custom software development?

Custom software development is building software designed specifically for your business instead of using an off-the-shelf product. BuildDeploy Tech designs and builds web, business and cloud software around your exact requirements.

When should a business build custom software?

Custom software usually makes sense when off-the-shelf tools don't fit your process, when teams rely on manual workarounds or disconnected systems, or when you need specific integrations and control. If a standard product already covers your needs, that's often faster and cheaper.

What kinds of custom software do you build?

We build web applications, business management software, admin dashboards and portals, APIs and integrations, workflow automation, database-driven systems, cloud applications and mobile apps.

Can custom software integrate with our existing systems and APIs?

Yes. We build and integrate APIs so your new software connects with the tools, databases and services you already use, which reduces duplicate data entry.

Which technologies do you use?

We work with technologies such as React, Node.js, Laravel, PHP, Flutter, MySQL, AWS, Docker and WordPress, chosen to fit each project rather than a fixed stack.

How do you estimate project cost and timeline?

Cost and timeline depend on the scope — features, number of user roles, integrations, data migration, platform and hosting needs, and ongoing support. We scope your requirements first and then share an estimate; we don't quote a fixed price or delivery date before understanding the project.

Do you provide deployment and maintenance after launch?

Yes. We deploy the software to the cloud and provide ongoing support and maintenance, and can keep improving and scaling it as your business grows.

Can you modernize or rebuild an existing application?

Yes. We can rebuild and modernize ageing software into a more secure, maintainable system, and migrate your existing data as part of the project.

How do I request a consultation or quote?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech through the contact page or WhatsApp with a short description of what you need, and the team will scope your project and share a quote.
