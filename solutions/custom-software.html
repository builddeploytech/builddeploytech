FAQ

Custom software questions, answered

What is custom software development?

Custom software development is building software designed specifically for your business instead of using an off-the-shelf product. BuildDeploy Tech designs and builds web, business and cloud software around your exact requirements.

What kinds of custom software do you build?

We build web applications, business management software, admin dashboards and portals, APIs and integrations, workflow automation, database-driven systems, cloud applications and mobile apps.

Which technologies do you work with?

We work with technologies such as React, Node.js, Laravel, PHP, Flutter, MySQL, AWS, Docker and WordPress, chosen to fit each project rather than a fixed stack.

Can you integrate with our existing systems and APIs?

Yes. We build and integrate APIs so your new software connects with the tools, databases and services you already use.

Do you provide support and maintenance after launch?

Yes. We support and maintain the software after launch and can keep improving and scaling it as your business grows.

How do I get started or get a quote?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech through the contact page with a short description of what you need, and the team will scope your project and share a quote.
