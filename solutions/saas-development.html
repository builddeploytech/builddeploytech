FAQ

SaaS development questions, answered

What is SaaS product development?

SaaS (Software as a Service) product development is building cloud-hosted software that customers access by subscription. BuildDeploy Tech designs and builds scalable, multi-tenant SaaS products from idea to launch.

Can you build a multi-tenant SaaS platform?

Yes. Multi-tenancy is core to how we build SaaS — one secure codebase serves many customers with each tenant's data cleanly isolated. Our own BDT Retail platform is built this way.

Do you handle subscriptions, billing and payments?

Yes. We build recurring subscription plans, upgrades, invoicing and payment-gateway integration directly into your SaaS product.

Which technologies do you use for SaaS?

We typically build SaaS with technologies such as Laravel, Node.js, React, MySQL, Redis, Docker and AWS, chosen to fit each product rather than a fixed stack.

Have you built a SaaS product yourselves?

Yes. BDT Retail is our own multi-tenant, cloud-hosted SaaS platform for billing and retail management, so we build client SaaS products on real, hands-on experience.

How do I start building my SaaS product?

Contact BuildDeploy Tech with a short description of your idea, and the team will help scope a first version and share a quote.
