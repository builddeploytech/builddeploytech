Yes — with cart, payment gateway, order and inventory management and SEO. Ecommerce projects start from ₹24,999 depending on features.

Do you develop mobile apps?

Yes — Android and Flutter apps with REST APIs and backend. Mobile app projects start from ₹49,999 depending on scope.

BDT Retail is our cloud billing, POS and inventory software. Plans start at ₹299/month (Starter) and ₹999/month (Premium), with annual plans at ₹2,999 and ₹9,999.

Yes — SEO from ₹7,500/month and website maintenance from ₹2,999/month, covering optimization, security, updates and support.
