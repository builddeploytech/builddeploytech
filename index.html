Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about BuildDeploy Tech, BDT Retail and our services.

What does BuildDeploy Tech do?

BuildDeploy Tech is a software development company in Noida that builds SaaS products like BDT Retail, custom software solutions, and delivers technology services including web development, mobile apps, DevOps, cloud and SEO.

What is BDT Retail?

BDT Retail is BuildDeploy Tech's billing and business management software for retail businesses, covering billing and invoicing, POS, inventory management, GST reports and multi-store management.

Do you build custom software and SaaS products?

Yes. We build custom software, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, white-label SaaS and enterprise solutions tailored to your business requirements.

Which technology services does BuildDeploy Tech provide?

We provide web development, mobile app development, DevOps and cloud, SEO and digital marketing, WordPress development and government tender support.

Which industries can use BDT Retail billing software?

BDT Retail works for kirana and general stores, garments, footwear, electrical, hardware, cosmetics, mobile accessories, stationery, restaurants and wholesale or distribution businesses.

Where is BuildDeploy Tech located?

BuildDeploy Tech is based in Noida and serves clients across Delhi NCR and India.
